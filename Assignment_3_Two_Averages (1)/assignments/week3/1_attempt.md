Causal claims (C)

C: The data does not establish that inflation, government policy, transport costs or another particular factor caused the price changes.
C: The data does not prove that every shop or every household paid the same prices.
C: A rise between the first and last observations does not prove that prices increased continuously on every date.

Referee's decision

The mean and median should both be reported because they describe different features of the data. The mean uses every value and can be affected by extreme values. The median identifies the middle observation and is less affected by extreme values. The first-to-last percentage change gives a direct comparison between the two endpoints, but it does not describe every movement in between. I would therefore avoid declaring one average correct in every situation. The best measure depends on the shape of the data and the question being asked.
